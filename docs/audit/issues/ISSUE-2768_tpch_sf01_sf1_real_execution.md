## Decision: Document, Don't Block v3.8.0 GA

The 4 parse errors and 3 simplified queries are **known limitations** of v3.8.0
parser. They are not regressions — they are scope decisions made in the
v3.8.0 design. Documenting them here is sufficient; fixing them is a
v3.9.0+ concern.

The Q1 SF1 timing (14.93s) being 2× slower than MySQL (7.08s) is
**expected for an immature OLAP engine without vectorized execution, JIT
compilation, or columnar storage**. Tracking the gap over time as a
metric is more useful than trying to "fix" it for GA.
